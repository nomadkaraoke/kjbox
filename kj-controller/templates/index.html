Device HDA Name Jack IEC958

Rewrites /etc/asound.conf so the hdmiout ALSA alias points to this device. Use when the auto-detected device is wrong. Temporary — reset on restart.

ALSA device used by the karaoke player (mpv/VLC) and filler music. hdmiout (ALSA alias) is correct for normal HDMI operation; pick hw:0,0 to route everything through the 3.5mm jack. Persisted across restarts.
